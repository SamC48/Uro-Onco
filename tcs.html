General Information

Uro-Onco Congress 2027 is scheduled for 28–30 May 2027.

The congress will be hosted at NH Johannesburg Sandton, in Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa.

The congress is intended for urologists, oncologists, healthcare professionals, registrars, medical students, researchers and industry partners interested in uro-oncology.

Registration & Fees

Registration information and the booking link will be available on the congress website.

Final registration fees will be published once confirmed. Different delegate categories may have different rates.

The final list of registration inclusions, including any networking functions or additional charges, will be published with the registration packages.

Programme & CPD

The scientific programme and session information will be published on the congress Programme page.

Details of confirmed CPD accreditation and any applicable Ethics points will be published before the congress.

Venue & Accommodation

NH Johannesburg Sandton offers hotel accommodation. Delegates should confirm availability and booking arrangements directly with the hotel or congress organisers.

NH Johannesburg Sandton is located in Sandton, Johannesburg. Please refer to the venue information on the congress website for directions and additional details.

Sponsorship

The congress offers Platinum and Gold sponsorship opportunities, together with other partnership initiatives described in the sponsorship prospectus.

Organisations interested in supporting Uro-Onco Congress 2027 can review the sponsorship prospectus and contact the organising committee regarding available opportunities.
